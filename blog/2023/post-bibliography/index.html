References

  1. RLC
    Intrinsic Closed-Loop Practical Asymptotic Stability in Discrete-Time Reinforcement Learning
    Jan de Priester and Ricardo G. Sanfelice
    In Reinforcement Learning Conference (RLC), 2026
    Accepted, to appear
    Article Abs PDF

    Deep reinforcement learning (RL) has achieved impressive empirical success in complex control tasks, many of which are fundamentally set-stabilization problems. While existing methods often modify RL algorithms to enforce strict asymptotic stability, we offer a complementary perspective: modifying the core RL objective is often unnecessary to guarantee the stability of the deployed policy. We prove that the unmodified discounted RL objective intrinsically guarantees closed-loop uniform practical asymptotic stability (UPAS) in deterministic continuous control settings. By adapting the discrete-time input-to-state stability (ISS) framework, we model the suboptimality induced by the discount factor and neural network approximation error as bounded disturbances, proving that near-optimal parameterized feedback laws steer the closed-loop system into a bounded neighborhood of the target set that vanishes as approximation error approaches zero and the discount factor approaches one.

  2. RLC
    A Timer-Based Hybrid Supervisor for Robust, Chatter-Free Policy Switching
    Jan de Priester and Ricardo G. Sanfelice
    In Reinforcement Learning Conference (RLC), 2025
    Article Abs PDF

    We address the challenge of switching among multiple learned policies in reinforcement learning control systems, where conventional value function-based selection can induce rapid chattering under small measurement noise. We propose a predictive timer-based hybrid supervisor that integrates a resettable timer enforcing an adaptive dwell time on the active policy by predicting forward trajectories, rendering the target set robustly globally asymptotically stable.

  3. ACC
    Hysteresis-Based RL: Robustifying Reinforcement Learning-based Control Policies via Hybrid Control
    Jan de Priester, Ricardo G. Sanfelice, and Nathan Van De Wouw
    In 2022 American Control Conference (ACC), 2022
    Abs DOI PDF

    Combines hybrid control theory with reinforcement learning by embedding hysteresis-based switching logic across overlapping state-space regions, eliminating chattering vulnerabilities in obstacle avoidance tasks.
